Before answering anything about their setup, installed tools, or files, go read them.

**Why:** Second-best-evidenced pattern in the 2026-09-09 history mine — two instances across
two projects: "you are not lookign at my config there is no expansion" and "my .claude is
empty what are you talkign about". Both times I answered from assumption and was wrong.

**How to apply:** Run the check first, then answer. Cheap to verify, and being wrong about
their own machine is the failure they call out most sharply. Also removed from the config on
2026-09-09 when the Karpathy file replaced the measured one. Related: [[prefers-short-answers]].
